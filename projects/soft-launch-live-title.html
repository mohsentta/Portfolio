Live-ops on Floodia, a soft-launched mobile title from Lazy Samurai: keeping the build healthy while features kept landing.

ABOUT THE GAME

Floodia is a recreation of Snakes and Ladders, rebuilt around a story: a mysterious island with pirate-flavoured characters. You play as Alia, whose ship is wrecked on an unknown shore. As she meets the island's other inhabitants, she uncovers why each of them is cursed — and why none of them can leave.
